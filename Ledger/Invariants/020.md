# I020

## Predicate

A new installation records each shipped file's checksum, executable flag and ownership correctly. The manifest does not checksum itself, and later edits do not alter its recorded baseline.

## Oracle

test_installation_manifest_records_ownership in tooling/worker/src/scaffold/package/tests/test_manifest.py initializes a real native consumer, compares every entry with installed bytes and mode, checks ownership categories, then edits a skill and observes the checksum mismatch.
