# I009

## Predicate

The memory accepts no cards, one card and cards that After lines order after existing cards, and rejects a number two cards hold, an After line naming a missing card or a name other than a card name, and After lines forming a cycle.

## Oracle

Configured cargo target `project_memory::tests::plan::plan_delivery_contract` verifies this behavior through the native runtime. D242 revises the predicate for the cards that replace the Plan index; the target and the marked test keep their names.
