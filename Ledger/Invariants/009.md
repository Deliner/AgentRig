# I009 — Adaptive feature contract

## Predicate

Under [D012](../Decisions/012.md), the Plan accepts idle and paused delivery and allows at most one active feature. Rows and details must follow the documented schema. Prerequisites exist, are unique and acyclic, and are complete before a dependent feature is active or complete. Paused and complete details contain Delivery context. D012 replaces the former exactly-one-active predicate while retaining outcome-based feature contracts.

## Oracle

[test_plan_delivery_contract](../../tooling/tests/test_plan_policy.py) checks valid delivery states and rejection of malformed contracts and dependencies. Companion lifecycle tests exercise a discovered prerequisite, queued follow-up, completion, and resumption. The oracle checks structural consistency; semantic acceptance remains the executing agent's responsibility.
