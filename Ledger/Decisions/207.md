# D207 — `silent-error` reports `.ok().flatten()` and a fallback after `.join()`

## Context

B163 under D151. The voxel-rust review at `2304625` found two erasures that `silent-error` did not report. Template
6: `.ok().flatten()` turns `Result<Option<T>, E>` into `Option<T>` and loses the error, at `src/browser/legacy.rs:33`,
where an invalid saved seed becomes seed 41, and in `src/browser/store.rs`. Template 4: the result of a worker
thread's `join` is replaced by a default value, so the thread's panic becomes an empty part, in three probes and in
two calls of a method `Simulation::join` in tests. The card asks for an error of `silent-error` on `.ok().flatten()`
and on a `.join()` without arguments followed by `.unwrap_or*` or `.ok()`; the skill propagates the error or records
the reason with a `DECISION` reference.

## Chosen

`silent-error` and its skill `handle-error` extend instead of a new kind. The rule reports every Rust `.flatten()`
call without arguments whose receiver is a `.ok()` call without arguments, and every Rust `.ok()` or
`.unwrap_or_default()` call without arguments, or `.unwrap_or(..)` or `.unwrap_or_else(..)` call with one, whose
receiver is a `.join()` call without arguments; outside test code and without a `DECISION:` or `INVARIANT:` reference
on the line directly above, as for the other Rust forms of the rule. `.unwrap_or*` reads as these three standard
methods. The finding names the outer method. The skill propagates the error of a `Result<Option<T>, E>` with `?`
before taking the option, and lets a joined thread's panic continue through a `match` with `resume_unwind` or turns it
into an error with `.map_err(..)?`.

The rig's own module walk of D144, which proceeds past declarations it cannot resolve, keeps its two
`.ok().flatten()` chains with a `DECISION: D144` reference each.

## Rejected

- A new kind: the category, level, repair and justification are those of `silent-error`.
- Reporting the new forms in test code while the rule's other Rust forms skip it: one rule would then have two
  scopes. A test module in its own file, as voxel-rust's `tests/` files, carries no test attribute in that file and is
  reported anyway.
- Resolving the receiver's type to tell `JoinHandle::join` from another `join` without arguments, such as
  `Simulation::join`: the rig resolves no types, and std has no other `join` without arguments, so another one is a
  project's own method whose fallback the review counted as the same hazard.
- Exempting `.unwrap_or_else(..)` whose closure calls `resume_unwind`: the card names every `.unwrap_or*`, and the
  skill writes the re-raise as a `match`, which the rule does not report.
- A `.join()` bound to a variable or passed through `?` before the fallback: the card takes the direct chain, the form
  the review found.

## Rationale

Both forms erase an error or a panic inside one expression, so syntax sees the whole loss at one site, and the repair
and its justification are those `silent-error` already asks for.

## Consequences

The extension applies wherever `silent-error` is configured as soon as a runtime carrying it is pinned (D051). In
voxel-rust it reports the review's sites, the two `Simulation::join` fallbacks in its out-of-line test modules
included.
