# D107 — Dev-gate Clippy C profile: five more denied lints and crate-level `unsafe` confinement

## Context

B060 (research arc: the user's decision of 2026-11-02 item 3 and steps 2 and 6 of the coverage map),
selected as P065 under the /goal of 2026-11-04. D060 made Clippy this repository's development gate
with a panic-risk deny set and D068 extended it with `let_underscore_must_use`. The arc named five
further restriction/pedantic lints with a near-zero false-positive profile on AgentRig's code, and an
`unsafe` confinement: every `unsafe` site of the product lives under `command_runner/jobs` (process
groups, identity, streams). Baseline on master `4cf520c`: the five lints produced exactly one finding,
`self_named_module_files` on `structural_lint/rules.rs` beside `structural_lint/rules/`, contrary to
the card's expectation of zero repair.

## Chosen

The clippy profile in `.agentrig/commands.yaml` additionally denies `clippy::exit`,
`clippy::should_panic_without_expect`, `clippy::missing_fields_in_debug`,
`clippy::rest_pat_in_fully_bound_structs` and `clippy::self_named_module_files`, one VAC per lint,
each verified by a violating fragment that fails the profile and passes after removal. `Project/lib.rs`
carries `#![deny(unsafe_code)]`; the single `#[allow(unsafe_code)]` sits on the `pub mod jobs;`
declaration in `command_runner/mod.rs` with this decision's reference on the line above (D080), so
`unsafe` outside `command_runner::jobs` fails to compile. `structural_lint/rules.rs` moved to
`rules/mod.rs`; because the move placed its catalog re-exports inside `rules/`, which `catalog` already
reaches through `languages`, the re-exports were removed, consumers call `catalog::` directly, and the
`rules` dependents budget is raised to its measured radius of 7 (D079). `derived_hash_with_manual_eq`
and `derive_ord_xor_partial_ord` were verified deny-by-default in clippy 1.98 (`-W help`) and are not
added. Not selected: `wildcard_enum_match_arm`, `map_err_ignore`, `cast_*` (user decision of
2026-11-02) and `allow_attributes_without_reason` (duplicates D080). Extends D060 and D068.

## Rejected

`--all-targets` for the profile (D060: test code keeps `unwrap`/`expect`); an
`#[allow(clippy::self_named_module_files)]` on `rules.rs` (the lint's one real finding is exactly the
mixed style it forbids); `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` (no justified subtree would be possible); a second
clippy run for test targets (a new mechanism for one lint); shipping the profile through `init`
(D060: external tools fall outside the portable install).

## Rationale

Each lint names a defect class the arc found undetectable by the syntactic engine (a hidden process
exit, a panicking test without an expectation, a manual `Debug` that hides fields, a `..` pattern
masking a future field, mixed module styles), and the five cost zero repair except the one real
mixed-style pair, which the move fixed without changing any public path. Confining `unsafe` to one
declared subtree turns the arc's observation that all `unsafe` lives in `jobs` into a compile-time
contract with one justified allow (D080) instead of a review discipline. The policy stays one gate's
configuration (D060).

## Consequences

The commit gate rejects `process::exit` outside `main`, a `#[should_panic]` without `expected`, a
manual `Debug` omitting fields, a fully bound struct pattern with `..`, a `foo.rs` beside `foo/`, and
`unsafe` outside `command_runner::jobs`. Limitation recorded: the profile runs without `--all-targets`
(D060), so `should_panic_without_expect` sees only attributes in non-test code; AgentRig's own tests
under `#[cfg(test)]` are not linted by it. `rules::` no longer forwards catalog items; `catalog::` is
the owner's path. The `init` delivery is unchanged (I020). Supersedes nothing.
