# D129 — Error cause and field visibility lints join the dev-gate profile

## Context

B081. On 2026-11-07 the user asked to enable Clippy lints that catch smells from the rule lists they had submitted. With
clippy 1.98.1, the gate's pin, a run on master `cb258b7` gave three `map_err_ignore` sites, three
`partial_pub_fields` types and no `struct_excessive_bools`, `mutex_atomic` or `mutex_integer` hit. D107 had not
selected `map_err_ignore` on 2026-11-02 without measuring it. Item 5 of `type-driven-discipline` allowed some fields of
a struct to stay `pub` while validated fields were private.

## Chosen

The clippy command in `.agentrig/commands.yaml` denies `clippy::map_err_ignore`, `clippy::partial_pub_fields` and
`clippy::struct_excessive_bools`, one VAC per lint, each proven by a fragment appended to the crate root that fails the
catalog clippy and passes once removed.

The two thread joins in `command_runner/jobs` report the panic text of the joined thread through `panic_text` beside
`join` in `streams.rs`. `Display` for the rule `Kind` keeps `map_err(|_| fmt::Error)` under
`#[allow(clippy::map_err_ignore)]` with this reference (D080): `fmt::Error` carries no payload, and serializing a unit
variant of the kebab-case enum does not fail.

`Session` in `agent_hooks/reminder.rs`, `Declaration` in `structural_lint/architecture/policy.rs` and `Selector` in
`structural_lint/selection` become abstractions with every field private and accessors for what consumers read: the
session holds its lock and path while its state is changed, a declaration's purpose is validated on load, and a
selector's compiled overrides follow the rule's override order. Item 5 of `type-driven-discipline` states that a struct
is either a record with every field `pub` or an abstraction with every field private.

## Rejected

`mutex_atomic` and `mutex_integer`: product code has no lock to repair, an atomic forces a memory-ordering choice that
D108 treats as a decision of its own, the corpus triage found 5 of 12 `Mutex<bool>` or `Mutex<integer>` sites
replaceable, and a mutex is the safer default for code maintained for 20 years. Making the three types records with
every field `pub`: each holds an invariant between its fields. Hiding the `Kind` error behind a hand-written kebab-case
conversion: a second copy of the serde rename rule.

## Rationale

A joined thread's panic text is the only cause the caller can report, and dropping it leaves "thread failed" with no
way to find the panic. A struct that mixes open and closed fields hides from its declaration whether consumers may
build or change it freely. A struct with many `bool` fields is a set of modes or states that the norm expects as an
`enum`.

## Consequences

`map_err(|_| …)` in product code fails the gate unless the closure names its argument; a named but unused argument
such as `|_error|` passes, as Clippy documents. A struct mixing `pub` and non-`pub` fields fails, while `pub(crate)` and
`pub(super)` count as non-`pub`. A struct with more than three `bool` fields fails. The profile stays this
repository's dev gate here; B082 decides delivery. It extends D060 and D127 and supersedes nothing.
