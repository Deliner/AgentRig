# D171 — Variants of B122: `hidden-exit`, `untyped-conversion` and `stale-check` ship at error level

## Context

D168 did not ship `hidden-exit` (IV-20) at 40 of 46 and `untyped-conversion` (IV-22) at 57 of 64 checked findings,
and D170 did not ship `stale-check` (IV-30) at 26 of 41. Each rejected narrowing a detector against the measured
sites. B122 under D151 therefore treats a variant as a new detector: AC1 drops macros whose name split at underscores
has a part `bail`, `ensure`, `require`, `return`, `try`, `break`, `continue`, `exit`, `throw`, `fail` or `propagate`,
a list in the verdict text of P123 before its freeze; AC2 drops an `.into()` on a parameter bounded by `Into`; AC6
keeps only a check after which absence is normal and a later action on the same path propagates its error with `?`.
AC3 measures each variant by the protocol of P120 with the earlier verdict texts on a new corpus sample that excludes
the sites of the earlier sample, and ships it only at 90% or more. The protocol and predicates were committed in
`4179ce3` before commit `9e9af8b` froze them as three inert kinds; the measurement is recorded in `28f494b`.

## Chosen

The three kinds join `catalog::ALL` and the Rust handler at error level outside test code as recognized by D148, each
silenced by a `DECISION:` or `INVARIANT:` reference on the line directly above, and `init` ships them with their
repair skills:

- `hidden-exit`: every `return`, `?`, and `break` or `continue` outside a loop of the same body in a `macro_rules!`
  transcriber outside a closure, nested `fn` or async block, unless the macro name has an exit word part. The skill
  `expose-the-exit` writes the exit at the call site through a function returning `Result` or `Option`.
- `untyped-conversion`: every `let` of a plain name without a type over `.parse()`, `.into()`, `.try_into()`, `.sum()`,
  `.product()` or `.collect()` without turbofish, `Default::default()` or `From::from(..)`, except an `.into()` on a
  plain parameter name of the nearest `fn` typed `impl` with an `Into` bound or by a generic parameter of that `fn`,
  `impl` or trait bounded by `Into`. The skill `name-the-target` writes the type on the `let` or as a turbofish.
- `stale-check`: an `exists()`, `is_file()` or `is_dir()` check governing an `if`, directly or through a plain `let`,
  whose absence branch is missing or holds no error exit and no file action, followed in the same `fn` by an `fs` or
  `File::open`/`File::create` call on the same path text, in the presence branch or after an absence branch that
  leaves, whose error propagates through `?`. The skill `act-and-match-the-error` performs the action and matches
  its error kind.

`expose-the-exit` and `act-and-match-the-error` sit in `control-flow`, `name-the-target` in `signature`. The ten rig
findings of `untyped-conversion` are repaired with a type on the `let`; the rig has no `hidden-exit` or `stale-check`
finding. The Clippy profile is unchanged.

## Rejected

Shipping a variant below the bar: none fell below it, so no variant is removed.

Judging a variant on the earlier sample or on the rig and voxel-rust: those sites shaped AC1, AC2 and AC6 and were
already labeled, so they cannot test the variant.

Narrowing a variant further against the sites of this measurement: the same sites would then both tune and judge it.

## Rationale

On the new corpus sample `hidden-exit` is true on 23 of 23, 100%, among 172 findings in 14 of 18 projects;
`untyped-conversion` on 47 of 48, 98%, among 1196 in 18 of 18; `stale-check` on 15 of 16, 94%, among 59 in 8 of 18. No
project had more than three checked sites. Each variant is a subset of its earlier detector: no tree has a finding
beyond the earlier ones. The false `untyped-conversion` site is an `.into()` on the result of calling a parameter
`context: impl FnOnce() -> C` with `C: Into<..>`, where the bound fixes the target but the exception reads only a
parameter name. The false `stale-check` site is a file included as a `#[cfg(test)] mod` from another file, which D148
does not see as test code; by the verdict text on the race the finding is true, which gives 16 of 16.

## Consequences

The kinds apply wherever they are configured as soon as a runtime carrying them is pinned (D051). The frozen
predicates remain in commit `9e9af8b`, and the counts, classes and examples are in the delivery of P127. Of the earlier
false sites the new predicates keep only the helix `.into()` on a loop variable of a type bounded by `Into`, and a
conversion through a called parameter stays reported. voxel-rust has 3 `hidden-exit`, 3 `untyped-conversion` and 1
`stale-check` findings, which are reported and not repaired. D171 supersedes the rejection of `hidden-exit` and
`untyped-conversion` in D168 and of `stale-check` in D170; their other choices stand.
