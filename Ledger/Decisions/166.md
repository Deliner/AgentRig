# D166 — Type states of B116: a positional `bool` and a returned guard ship, a map to `bool` does not

## Context

B116 under D151 takes three forms of list IV of 2026-11-11: IV-6 a `bool` among two or more positions of a tuple
struct or tuple variant; IV-7 a `bool` as the value of a `HashMap`, `BTreeMap`, `IndexMap` or `DashMap`, planned as an
extension of `ambiguous-option`; and IV-10 a `MutexGuard`, `RwLockReadGuard`, `RwLockWriteGuard`, `Ref` or `RefMut` in
the return type of a `pub` function. AC1 ships a rule only when at least 90% of its checked findings are true, by the
protocol of D165 recorded in the delivery of P120: frozen copies of the rig, voxel-rust and the 18 open Rust projects of
corpus r8, a fixed sample, a finding true when the card's hazard applies and its «Оставить» category does not, an
undecidable finding false. The verdict clauses of each form were committed in `5da2504` before the predicates were
frozen. A probe of Clippy 1.98.1 with defaults, the rig profile with `max-struct-bools = 1`, and all pedantic and
nursery lints reported only a tuple struct of two `bool` values, through `struct_excessive_bools`.

## Chosen

IV-6 becomes the error-level kind `positional-flag` with the repair skill `name-the-flag` in the naming group: every
Rust tuple struct or tuple enum variant of two or more fields, one of them of type exactly `bool`, outside test code,
without a `DECISION:` or `INVARIANT:` reference on the line directly above it or its attributes. `Option<bool>` in a
position stays with `ambiguous-option`, and `&bool` is not counted. The detector shares the tuple pass of
`same-type-positions`, so `(bool, bool)` gives a finding of each kind, and Clippy's `struct_excessive_bools` reports
such a struct as well; the card accepts the duplicates.

IV-10 becomes the error-level kind `exposed-guard` with the repair skill `scope-the-guard` in the ownership group:
every Rust `fn` with a visibility modifier, `pub`, `pub(crate)`, `pub(super)` or `pub(in ..)`, whose return type
contains at any depth a generic type whose last name is exactly `MutexGuard`, `RwLockReadGuard`, `RwLockWriteGuard`,
`Ref` or `RefMut`, from any path, outside test code, without a reference on the line directly above the `fn` or its
attributes. A trait method and its implementations carry no visibility modifier and are not reported. A lock wrapper
whose API hands out its guard has the same hazard and takes a reference.

## Rejected

IV-7, the extension of `ambiguous-option` to maps of `bool` or `AtomicBool`: 16 of 19 sampled corpus findings are
true, 84%, among 45 added corpus findings and none on the rig or voxel-rust. Its false findings are snapshots of a
foreign format where a missing entry and `false` both mean off, as vaultwarden's `featureStates` for Bitwarden clients
and zed's copy of an agent profile's tool settings, or are undecidable, as rustdesk's map serialized for its Dart UI.
Syntax cannot tell such a snapshot from internal state. `ambiguous-option` stays as D164 sets it.

A public field `pub inner: Mutex<..>` from IV-10's other places: the caller writes `lock()` itself, so the lock is
visible at its site. Resolving `Ref` and `RefMut` to `std::cell` through imports: it is a different rule with its own
coverage and needs its own agreement and measurement. Narrowing either detector against the measured sites: the same
sites would then both tune and judge it.

## Rationale

IV-6 passed with 26 of 26 sampled findings true among 56 corpus findings: «Оставить» names only a single-field
struct, which the predicate excludes. IV-10 passed with 22 of 24 sampled findings true among 107 corpus findings; both
false ones are Bevy's change-detection `Ref`, which holds no cell borrow. It passes by one finding. Outside the sample,
23 of Bevy's 37 findings are its `Ref` or the associated type `F::Ref`, so precision over the whole population is
lower than the sample's, and 11 of the 22 true findings are lock wrappers whose guard is their purpose; the skill tells
both to take a reference. Real hazards remain: zed's `MultiBuffer::read` returns a `Ref` while a second call borrows
the same cell mutably, and cargo's `shell()` returns a `MutexGuard` that two calls in one expression deadlock on.

## Consequences

Both kinds apply wherever they are configured once a runtime carrying them is pinned (D051), and `init` ships them
with their skills; the rig and voxel-rust had no site. The frozen IV-7 predicate remains in commit `90a917a`, and the
measured counts, classes and examples are in the delivery of P121. IV-7 remains a review norm in list IV; a later
decision may add a detector with a different predicate measured anew.
