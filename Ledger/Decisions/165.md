# D165 — Error loss of B115: the `filter_map(ok)` form ships, `if let Ok` without `else` and text branches do not

## Context

B115 under D151 takes three forms of list IV of 2026-11-11: IV-2 `filter_map` or `flat_map` that keeps only the `Ok`
values of any chain, which `silent-error` flags only after `read_dir` since D097; IV-3 an `if let Ok(..)` without
`else`, where `try_*` methods whose `Err` means busy or empty stay allowed; and IV-4 a branch on the text of an error
through `to_string()` and `contains`, `starts_with` or `ends_with`. AC1 ships a rule only when at least 90% of its
checked findings are true, measured by a protocol recorded in the delivery of P120 before any run: frozen copies of the
rig, voxel-rust and the 18 open Rust projects of corpus r8, a fixed sample, test code excluded, a finding true when the
card's hazard applies at the site and its «Оставить» category does not, an undecidable finding false. Each form has one
owner, and Clippy runs only on the rig and voxel-rust. A probe of Clippy 1.98.1 defaults on 2026-11-11 showed which
spellings Clippy already reports.

## Chosen

IV-2 extends `silent-error` and its skill `handle-error` instead of adding a kind: every Rust `.filter_map(ok)` or
`.flat_map(ok)` on any chain, where `ok` is `Result::ok` or a closure of one parameter whose body is only a `.ok()`
call without arguments, outside test code, without a `DECISION:` or `INVARIANT:` reference on the line directly above
the chain. `Result::ok` and `|x| x.ok()` on a `.lines()` call stay with Clippy's default `lines_filter_map_ok`, and
`.map(Result::ok).flatten()` stays with `map_flatten`; `|line| line.parse().ok()` after `.lines()`, which Clippy does
not report, stays with the rig. Chains rooted at `read_dir` keep `.flatten()` and `.map_while(ok)` with the same `ok`.

The silent-error detector moves into the family `languages/rust/errors` beside the control-flow, signature and
ownership families. The rules directory's dependents budget rises from 12 to 13 and the measure directory's from the
default 6 to 7 for that new consumer of the rule identities and the shared measurements, as D155 did for control flow.

## Rejected

IV-3, a kind `unhandled-error`: 62% of 53 sampled corpus findings are true, 33 of 53, among 1984 corpus findings and
none on the rig or voxel-rust. Its false findings fall in classes syntax without types cannot tell from a lost error:
the `Result` is handled after the `if` or by the callee, as in uv's `if let Err(error) = result` on the next line; the
`Err` is the answer of a lookup or check, as Bevy's `Query::get` for an entity outside the query; the call is a parser
alternative or a message classification, as `try_parse`, nom and winnow backtracking. Only the first class has a
syntactic sign, a value read again after the `if`, and without it precision stays at 67%. Errors whose `Err` is an
answer, `binary_search`, `compare_exchange`, `fetch_update`, `try_unwrap` and `downcast`, were already exempt.
`let Ok(x) = e else { .. }` was rejected before the run: its `else` is mandatory and written by hand, so the skip is
visible.

IV-4, a kind `error-text-branch`: 1 of 5 corpus findings is true, coreutils `cp` matching `No such file or directory`
instead of `ErrorKind::NotFound`. The others branch on text where the error has no variant for the case, an `anyhow`
error or EMFILE without an `io::ErrorKind`, or read an error of a crate outside the corpus; the card keeps those.
`e.message().contains(..)` was rejected before the run: syntax cannot tell a foreign `message` method from error text.

Narrowing IV-3 to a fixed list of standard I/O and environment callees: it is a different rule with a different
coverage and needs its own agreement and measurement. Narrowing either detector against the measured sites: the same
sites would then both tune and judge it.

## Rationale

A rule that is wrong at more than one site in ten teaches agents to justify findings instead of repairing them, and a
protocol fixed before the run keeps the verdict independent of the detector's author. The IV-2 form passed with 39 of
39 sampled corpus findings true and needs no new kind: the category, level and repair are those of `silent-error`.
Leaving the Clippy spellings to Clippy keeps one finding per site.

## Consequences

The IV-2 extension applies wherever `silent-error` is configured as soon as a runtime carrying it is pinned (D051); the
rig and voxel-rust had no site. The frozen IV-3 and IV-4 predicates remain in commit `b235701`, and the measured
counts, classes and examples are in the delivery of P120. IV-3 and IV-4 remain review norms in list IV; a later
decision may add a detector with a different predicate measured anew.
