# D167 — Shared-state forms of B117 are not shipped

## Context

B117 under D151 takes three forms of list IV of 2026-11-11: IV-15 an atomic `store` whose argument reads the same
atomic through `load`; IV-16 a `wait` or `wait_timeout` with a guard outside a `while` or `loop`; and IV-17 a call to
`env::set_var`, `env::remove_var`, `env::set_current_dir`, `panic::set_hook`, `log::set_logger` or
`set_global_default` outside `main`. AC1 ships a rule only when at least 90% of its checked findings are true, by the
protocol of D165 recorded in the delivery of P120. The verdict clauses were committed in `c6e364c` before commit
`17fadab` froze the predicates as the inert kinds `load-then-store`, `unlooped-wait` and `process-setting`. A probe of
Clippy 1.98.1 with defaults, the rig profile, and all pedantic and nursery lints reported none of the forms.

## Chosen

None of the three forms becomes a rule kind. The inert kinds are removed, so Project equals master `6468e86` again and
the pinned runtime stays (D051). The forms remain review norms in list IV.

## Rejected

`load-then-store`: 0 of 3 corpus findings are true, all in bun, each with a single writer: under a lock taken above, by
a documented precondition, or on task-local ids.

`unlooped-wait`: 1 of 16 sampled findings among 24 in the corpus is true, 6%, and at most 3 of 16 if the two
`parking_lot` waits count by the letter of the verdict, although `parking_lot` 0.12 documents that its `Condvar` has no
spurious wakeups. Most findings wait on a process, an event or a channel; the others are `Condvar` wrappers whose
callers loop, or a pause where any outcome is acceptable.

`process-setting`: 22 of 45 sampled findings among 200 in the corpus are true, 49%. Of the false ones, 14 are refuted
by code: startup helpers called only from `main` before threads, single-test binaries, tests under a common lock, an
uncompiled test data file and single-threaded launchers; 9 are undecidable. Counting all undecidable findings true
gives 69%.

Narrowing a detector against the measured sites: the same sites would then both tune and judge it.

## Rationale

The hazard of each form depends on facts the syntax does not show: how many writers an atomic has, whether the
receiver of `wait` is a condition variable, and whether other threads or unordered tests run when a process setting
changes. Real hazards exist: fish `src/threads/threads.rs:355` lets a spurious wakeup end a pool thread below its soft
minimum, and meilisearch tests set `TMPDIR` while sibling tests of the same binary create temporary directories. At
these precisions, however, a rule would mostly demand references on correct code.

## Consequences

No rule kind, skill or `lint.yaml` entry is added. The frozen predicates remain in commit `17fadab`, and the measured
counts, classes and examples are in the delivery of P122. A later decision may add a detector with a different
predicate measured anew.
