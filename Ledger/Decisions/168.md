# D168 — Visible evaluation of B118: `eager-fallback` covers `.or` and division, the other forms are not shipped

## Context

B118 under D151 takes forms of list IV of 2026-11-11: IV-18 an eager argument of `unwrap_or`, `ok_or`, `or` or
`map_or`, where a call or a macro goes to Clippy's `or_fun_call` in the rig profile, while `then_some` with a call and a
division in the argument extend `eager-fallback`; IV-19 an `$x:expr` fragment named twice in a `macro_rules!` body
outside `stringify!`; IV-20 `return`, `break`, `continue` or `?` in a `macro_rules!` body outside a closure and `fn`;
IV-22 a `let` without a type over `parse`, `into`, `try_into`, `collect`, `sum` or `product` without turbofish. AC1
ships a rule only when at least 90% of its checked findings are true, by the protocol of D165 recorded in the delivery
of P120. The verdict clauses were committed in `2715c93` before commit `3ee3318` froze the predicates: the new kinds
`repeated-fragment`, `hidden-exit` and `untyped-conversion` inert, and `eager-fallback` extended to `.or`,
`.then_some` and a division by a divisor that is not a literal. Before the run the delivery of P123 split the
extension by method into `then_some` and the four other methods, each judged separately. A probe of Clippy 1.98.1 with
defaults, the rig profile, and all pedantic and nursery lints reported only the calls and macros of `or_fun_call`.

## Chosen

`eager-fallback` flags every Rust `.unwrap_or`, `.ok_or`, `.map_or` or `.or` call whose eager first argument, outside a
closure in it, propagates with `?` or divides by a divisor that is not a literal, outside test code, without a
`DECISION:` or `INVARIANT:` reference on the line directly above it. Its skill `eager-to-lazy` adds `or_else`, the
division and the empty branch of a `match` or `let ... else` for a `?` that must leave the function. No rule kind,
skill or `lint.yaml` entry is added, `or_fun_call` stays out of the rig's Clippy profile, and the inert kinds are
removed.

## Rejected

`then_some` with a call or a macro: 6 of 39 sampled findings are true, 15%, among 3 on the rig and 229 in the corpus.
Most arguments are getters, constructors, `const fn`, wrappers of a reference such as `Path::new`, or clones of an
`Arc` or a handle, which the card keeps.

`or_fun_call` in the Clippy profile: 1 of 7 findings on the rig is true and voxel-rust has none; five of the false ones
are `unwrap_or(Path::new(..))`.

`repeated-fragment`: 17 of 43 sampled corpus findings are true, 40%, among 238 in the corpus and none on the rig or
voxel-rust. The false ones name the fragment in exclusive `if`, `match` or `cfg` branches, in separate bodies, or in
positions that are never evaluated, such as `#[doc]`, a type or `concat!`.

`hidden-exit`: 40 of 46 checked findings are true, 87%, 3 of 3 in voxel-rust and 37 of 43 in the corpus sample. All six
false findings are macros named after their exit and defined once, which the card keeps; the predicate does not read
the name. Counting Bevy's `try_range_inclusive` true gives 41 of 46, 89%.

`untyped-conversion`: 57 of 64 checked findings are true, 89%, 12 of 12 on the rig and voxel-rust and 45 of 52 in the
corpus sample. All seven false findings are `.into()` on a parameter bounded by `impl Into<X>` or `T: Into<X>`, where
the bound fixes the target.

Narrowing a detector against the measured sites: the same sites would then both tune and judge it.

## Rationale

A `?` in the eager argument of `.or` is the latent bug of D089: all 5 corpus findings are true, as uv
`crates/uv-settings/src/lib.rs:826`, where an invalid deprecated timeout variable aborts the run although the current
one is valid. A division by a divisor that is not a literal panics on zero on the value-present path; it has no site in
the frozen trees and ships under D151 with behavior tests. The other forms depend on facts these predicates do not
read: whether two uses share a path, whether a macro's name announces its exit, and whether a bound fixes the target of
a conversion.

## Consequences

The extension applies wherever `eager-fallback` is configured as soon as a runtime carrying it is pinned (D051); the
rig and voxel-rust had no site. The frozen predicates remain in commit `3ee3318`, and the measured counts, classes and
examples are in the delivery of P123. `then_some`, IV-19, IV-20 and IV-22 remain review norms in list IV; a later
decision may add a detector with a different predicate measured anew. D168 supersedes D089's restriction to
`.unwrap_or`, `.ok_or` and `.map_or` and to a `?`.
