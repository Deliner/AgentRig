# D001 — Separate decisions and invariants

## Context

Durable project knowledge includes both contextual choices and behavior that must always hold.

## Chosen

Record choices in the decision ledger and must-hold predicates in the invariant ledger with executable tests.

## Rejected

Use one undifferentiated policy log or encode rationale inside tests.

## Rationale

Choices and invariants fail differently and require different evidence.

## Consequences

Agents classify durable knowledge before recording it and split mixed claims.
