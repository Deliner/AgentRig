# D164 — A trait impl member's signature leaves `Option<Option<T>>` to its trait

## Context

B114. Under D163 `ambiguous-option` reported a nested option in the parameters, return type and `const` type of a trait
impl member, because the Clippy lint `option_option` skips those places. A trait impl takes those types from its trait,
so one unfortunate trait signature gave a Clippy error on the declaration and a rig finding on every implementation;
`impl From<Option<Option<u32>>> for X` gave two rig findings, on the header and on `from`; and the implementer of a
foreign trait could pass only with a reference. On 2026-11-11 the user chose that trait impl methods and `const` items
are not reported by the rig: the trait declaration or the impl header carries the finding.

## Chosen

`ambiguous-option` skips an `Option<Option<..>>` whose nearest enclosing fn or `const` item is a direct member of a trait
impl and which lies in that fn's parameter list or return type or in that `const`'s type, wherever it is nested there,
including inside a `fn` pointer, `dyn` or `impl` trait. Everything else stays as D163 sets it: the method body and the
`const` value, where a `let`, closure or turbofish is reported and a nested item is Clippy's; the impl header; an
associated type of the trait impl, which the implementer chooses; the method's own type parameters and `where` bounds,
which B114 keeps among the unchanged bound places; a fn or `static` of an extern block. `Option<bool>` is unchanged.

## Rejected

Dropping only the trait impl case from the places D163 compares with Clippy: a nested option inside a `fn` pointer,
`dyn` or `impl` trait in a trait impl signature would still be reported on every implementation, against B114 AC1.

## Rationale

The trait declaration or the impl header is the one place where the nested type is chosen, so the finding stays there;
a foreign trait gives no finding to an implementer who cannot change it.

## Consequences

A trait impl method whose bounds repeat a nested option of its trait still reports beside the trait. D164 supersedes
D163's inclusion of the parameters, return type and `const` type of a trait impl member; the rest of D163 remains.
