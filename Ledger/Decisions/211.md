# D211 — Report an index found in one collection and read from another

## Context

B168 under D151. The voxel-rust review at `2304625` found the priority of the touch zones encoded in two arrays:
`src/ui/touch/mod.rs` finds a position in a list of rectangles and then uses it as an index into a separate list of
roles. The pairing is correct today, but the order is written in two places, so a change to one list moves the other
list's values onto the wrong items. The card asks that the result of `.position(..)` on one collection, used as an
index of another collection, give a lint error, and that the skill keep the pairs together.

## Chosen

A Rust `parallel-index` lint kind at level error with the skill `keep-pairs-together` joins the naming detectors of
D156, which report values tied by position instead of a name. It reports an index expression, or a `.get(..)` or
`.get_mut(..)` call with one argument, whose index is the result of a one-argument `.position(..)` called directly on
`.iter()`, `.iter_mut()` or `.into_iter()` of a collection, when the text of the indexed collection differs from the
text of the searched one. The result counts when it is the index itself, also after `?` and method calls on it; when
it is a name bound from it by the pattern of `let`, `let .. else` or `if let`, in the statements after the `let` or
in the branch of the `if`; and when it is a parameter of a closure passed to a method called on it, in that closure's
body. Test code is skipped, and a `DECISION:` or `INVARIANT:` reference on the line directly above the read lifts the
finding. The finding stands on the line of the read with the symbol `position`. As a new kind, it is pinned before
the rig, the catalog and init enable it (D051).

## Rejected

- Every method named `position`: a project's own `position(x)` may search a field, so the indexed field is the same
  collection under other text; in voxel-rust `frame.position(resource)` finds an index of `frame.resources`.
- A `.position(..)` after `skip`, `rev`, `zip`, `enumerate` or `filter`: the index is shifted, reversed or belongs to
  several collections, so it is no plain index of the searched one.
- Following a rebinding to another name, a `match` on the result, a range or arithmetic around the index: each needs a
  data-flow analysis that the rig does not have; the binding by the same name and the closure parameter cover the
  review's site and the found ones.
- Resolving the collections by type or alias: receiver types are not resolved, as in D155 and D209; the text decides.

## Rationale

Two lists paired by order change correctly only together. One record per pair makes the pairing a type, so neither
value can move without the other and the search finds the record itself.

## Consequences

By a reading of the `.position(` sites of voxel-rust, the rule reports the review's read at `src/ui/touch/mod.rs:68`
and three more pairs of parallel lists: frame groups and layouts at `src/host/scene/layouts.rs:68` and
`src/host/scene/executor.rs:65`, and palette counts and entries at `src/core/world/section.rs:219`. The rig has no
such site. A rebinding to another name, a project's own `position` method, an adapted iterator, a range or an
arithmetic index stay unreported; an alias with other text for the searched collection is reported.
