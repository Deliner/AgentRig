# D181 — Five rig rules for forms of list V; twelve forms and the V-8 extension stay out

## Context

B132 under D151. List V of 2026-11-13 names forms that neither Clippy nor the rig detects and that a syntax tree finds
without types. The P136 protocol, recorded before the first run, froze the predicates of seventeen inert kinds and of
an extension of `blocking-in-async` (V-8) at `78736ba`. It measured them on the rig's `Project/` at `447eb73`, a copy of
voxel-rust's `src/` and the 18 projects of corpus r8. A sample of the findings was judged against the hazard of the
list's card and its "leave" clause. A kind ships when at least 90% of the judged findings are true overall and in each
group with 10 or more judged findings, and by D151 when no group has a finding.

## Chosen

Five Rust-only kinds at level error, each with a repair skill, delivered with a mid-branch pin (D051); init ships them
with every kind (I020). A finding stands at its line, and a `DECISION:` or `INVARIANT:` reference on the line directly
above lifts it.

| Kind | Form | Flags | Skill | Judged true |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `epsilon-tolerance` | V-3 | `f32::EPSILON` or `f64::EPSILON`, alone, in parentheses or negated, as a direct operand of `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=` or `==`, outside test code | `name-the-tolerance` | 20 of 20 |
| `trait-path-call` | V-12 | a call or path value `From::from`, `Into::into`, `TryFrom::try_from`, `TryInto::try_into` or `FromStr::from_str` without type arguments, outside a typed `let` and test code; the untyped `let` of a `From::from` call stays with `untyped-conversion` | `name-the-target` | 49 of 50 |
| `variant-list` | V-25 | an array of three or more paths naming every unit variant of an enum of the same file in declaration order, outside test code | `derive-the-variants` | 26 of 27 |
| `dotted-extension` | V-24 | `with_extension` or `set_extension` whose sole argument is a string literal starting with a dot, outside test code | `drop-the-dot` | no findings |
| `fixed-test-port` | V-28 | `TcpListener::bind` or `UdpSocket::bind` in test code with a literal nonzero port | `bind-port-zero` | no findings |

## Rejected

Twelve kinds and the V-8 extension fell below 90%. They were removed with their tests, and with them the detector
directories `unsafe_code` and `markers`.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| V-2 | `partial-order-fallback` | 5 of 11 | NaN filtered or ordered in the same function, strings compared |
| V-5 | `conditional-check` | 6 of 26 | the `if` selects table cases, or an unconditional check after the branch fails the test |
| V-7 | `unlooped-retry` | 1 of 4 | the retry loop stands at the caller |

A narrower predicate for a rejected form is a new card with a new measurement.

## Rationale

Each shipped form compiles and fails later: a tolerance that turns into exact equality above 2 and accepts unequal
values near zero, a conversion whose target changes with distant code, a list of variants that silently misses a new
one, a file name with two dots and tests that collide on one port. The rejected forms are mostly correct where they
stand, so a rule would demand references on correct code.

## Consequences

The rig's one finding, `.map(Into::into)` in `relative_imports.rs` whose `Cow<str>` target came from the vector, now
calls `Cow::from`. The `variant-list` message no longer suggests a const array checked by an exhaustive match, which
the rule still reports. Types are not resolved: `dotted-extension` also reports a method of the same name on another
type, and `trait-path-call` reports a result typed by a generic `T: FromStr`, its one false finding; both take a
reference. An enum with data variants is reported when the array names all of its unit variants. `fixed-test-port`
ships no exclude, since it measures only test code.
