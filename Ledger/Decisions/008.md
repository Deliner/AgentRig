# D008 — Separate product and durable knowledge

## Context

The worker environment must govern different product projects without mixing reusable process files into product code.

## Chosen

Keep all product contents under Project and place the Plan, Decisions, and Invariants indexes with their detail directories in the sibling Ledger directory.

## Rejected

Mix product and governance files at the repository root or place the Ledger inside the product directory.

## Rationale

Sibling roots give product work and durable worker knowledge distinct ownership while retaining one versioned environment.

## Consequences

Product implementation is rooted at Project, while agents resume and govern work through Ledger.
