# D003 — Edit checkpoint

## Context

Overlapping agent edits make review boundaries and rollback ambiguous.

## Chosen

After a content edit, block another edit until those contents are committed or reverted, while allowing correction after a failed commit.

## Rejected

Permit unlimited overlapping edits or require a commit after a no-op.

## Rationale

A single coherent diff is easier to verify and preserves unrelated work.

## Consequences

Codex edit hooks maintain checkpoint state under .git.
