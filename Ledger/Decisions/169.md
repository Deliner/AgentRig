# D169 — False pass of B119: an untargeted panic and a test substitution ship, the other forms do not

## Context

B119 under D151 takes four forms of list IV of 2026-11-11 about tests that pass falsely: IV-23 `#[should_panic]`
without `expected`; IV-24 `assert!(x.is_err())`; IV-25 `#[cfg(not(test))]` on an item; IV-26 `thread::sleep` in a
test. AC1 ships a rule only when at least 90% of its checked findings are true, by the protocol of D165 recorded in the
delivery of P120. IV-23, IV-24 and IV-26 are measured on test code and IV-25 outside it. The verdict clauses were
committed in `2428ac1` before commit `6965b25` froze the predicates as four inert kinds: `untargeted-panic`,
`untargeted-error`, `test-substitution` and `sleep-in-test`. A probe of Clippy 1.98.1 with `--tests`, the rig profile,
and all pedantic, nursery and restriction lints showed: the profile's `should_panic_without_expect` reports IV-23, but
the rig runs Clippy without `--tests`; the restriction lints `assertions_on_result_states` and `cfg_not_test` report
IV-24 and IV-25 and are not in the profile; no level reports `thread::sleep` in a test.

## Chosen

IV-23 becomes the error-level kind `untargeted-panic` with the repair skill `expect-the-panic`: every Rust attribute
whose path ends in `should_panic` with neither a `= ".."` value nor an `expected` argument, without a `DECISION:` or
`INVARIANT:` reference on the line directly above it. The attribute belongs to a test, so test code is in scope.

IV-25 becomes the error-level kind `test-substitution` with the repair skill `inject-the-variant`: every Rust
`#[cfg(..)]` or `#![cfg(..)]` attribute of an item in a file, module, `impl` or trait body whose predicate is false in
every build with `test` set, such as `not(test)`, `all(.., not(test), ..)` or `not(any(test, ..))`, outside test code
as recognized by D148, without such a reference. `cfg_attr`, `any(not(test), ..)`, statements and fields are not
counted.

Both kinds join `catalog::ALL` and the Rust handler, and `init` ships them. Their skills form the subgroup
`references/false-pass`, since one more directory in `references` would exceed its limit of 15. The Clippy profile is
unchanged, and the inert kinds `untargeted-error` and `sleep-in-test` are removed.

## Rejected

`untargeted-error`: 0 of 58 findings on the rig and voxel-rust are true. All 31 rig sites call functions returning
`anyhow::Result`, and the 27 voxel-rust sites return `&'static str`, `Box<dyn Error>` or an error turned into
`String`, which offer no case to compare. The corpus sample of 53 among 1700 findings was not judged: even all true it
would leave the overall precision at most 48%.

`sleep-in-test`: 7 of 32 sampled corpus findings are true, 22%, among 177 in 12 of 18 projects and none on the rig or
voxel-rust. Of the false ones, 14 pauses wait for the passage of real time the test checks, 4 sit in a polling loop
that exits on an event, and 7 do not affect the outcome.

`cfg_not_test` and `assertions_on_result_states` in the Clippy profile: B119 gives each form one owner, a rig rule.

Narrowing a detector against the measured sites: the same sites would then both tune and judge it.

## Rationale

`untargeted-panic`: 22 of 24 sampled corpus findings are true, 92%, among 175 in 11 of 18 projects and none on the rig
or voxel-rust. Any panic in the body passes such a test: helix `helix-lsp-types/src/semantic_tokens.rs:652` expects
«Length is not divisible by 5», but a mismatch in the `assert_eq!` after parsing would keep it green. The false ones
expect a standard library panic or one raised outside the corpus.

`test-substitution`: 12 of 13 sampled corpus findings are true, 92%, among 43 in 6 of 18 projects and none on the rig
or voxel-rust. uv `crates/uv-python-discovery/src/lib.rs:44` takes `std::env::current_dir` in the product and a
`cfg(test)` twin in tests, so no test runs the product path. The false one is compiled only with a test-support
feature that no product build enables.

`untargeted-error` and `sleep-in-test` depend on facts their predicates do not read: the error type of the called
function, and whether a pause orders threads or waits for real time.

## Consequences

Both kinds apply wherever they are configured as soon as a runtime carrying them is pinned (D051). The frozen
predicates remain in commit `6965b25`, and the measured counts, classes and examples are in the delivery of P124.
IV-24 and IV-26 remain review norms in list IV; a later decision may add a detector with a different predicate
measured anew. Tests written with `#[rstest]`, `#[test_case]` or inside `proptest!` are not recognized as test code,
as in D148.
