# D050 — Completed Backlog cards move to the archive

## Context

D031 rotates completed Plan rows and cards into Archive/Plan; the source Backlog
card stayed in Backlog and only gained a link to its Archive card. After all
selected cards were delivered, Backlog still listed them, and the user could
not tell which ideas were done. The user stated that completed cards must move
to the archive.

## Chosen

In the final VAC that archives a completed Plan outcome, move its source
Backlog row and card unchanged to Archive/Backlog.md and Archive/Backlog/NNN.md,
adjusting only relative links. Backlog lists only ideas that remain open.
memory-check validates the archived index and cards like the live Backlog and
rejects an ID present in both; ID allocation covers both.

## Rejected

Keep completed cards in Backlog with a status or completion link; delete them
and rely on Git; merge the card text into the archived Plan card; add a
separate archive command or registry.

## Rationale

The Plan archive already models completed history. Mirroring it keeps agreed
contracts readable at stable archive paths and leaves Backlog with the open
work. The existing memory checker remains the single owner of format checks.

## Consequences

B001, B002, B003, B008, B009, B014 and B018 move to the archive; archived Plan
cards and decision application links point to the archived cards. Archived IDs
stay allocated. This extends D031's rotation of completed Plan cards.
