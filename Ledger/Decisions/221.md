# D221 — Keep libc's deprecated Mach timebase binding for macOS CPU time

## Context

`pid_reuse_guard/macos.rs` converts the CPU time of a process from Mach ticks to nanoseconds with
`mach_timebase_info`. The libc crate marks its Mach bindings deprecated in favor of the `mach2` crate, so the
conversion carries `#[expect(deprecated)]`. Under D219 a lint exception names a decision given for its rule; D066,
which the site cited before, covers the soundness of the libc calls and not the use of a deprecated binding.

## Chosen

Keep the libc binding under `#[expect(deprecated)]` with the exception `DECISION: D221 allow-justification` on the
line above it. The binding still links the stable Mach call; when libc removes it, the build fails at this site and
the conversion moves to a replacement then.

## Rejected

- The `mach2` crate: a new dependency for one call.
- Reading `hw.tbfrequency` through `sysctlbyname`: it changes the arithmetic of a value no test checks, and the
  product of ticks and 10^9 needs wider integers.
- A private `extern` declaration of `mach_timebase_info`: it copies a binding libc owns and adds an FFI site to
  justify.

## Rationale

The deprecation announces a future removal, not a wrong result. Keeping the call preserves the current behavior, and
the compiler reports the removal when it happens, so nothing fails silently.

## Consequences

The exception in `macos.rs` lists this decision under Applies in; D066 keeps the `unsafe` exceptions of the same
file. A libc release without the binding fails the build until the conversion moves.
