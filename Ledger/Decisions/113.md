# D113 — State which jobs FFI sites check errors and the zeroed-struct precondition

## Context

B051 (self-findings of the cross-project review, agreed 2026-11-07). D066 justifies
the `command_runner/jobs` libc FFI under one soundness argument that includes
"checks the return value or `errno`", and D095 extends it to `libc::signal` in
`execute` with the same clause. Three sites in `jobs/process.rs` ignore the
result by design: `libc::signal(…, SIG_DFL)` in `restore_default_signals` and
the two `libc::kill` calls that forward a signal or terminate the group after a
failed attach. Restoring a default disposition and best-effort group termination
have no recovery path, so there is nothing to check; the prose over-promises.
The checking sites — `group.rs` (`== 0` with `ensure!`), the macOS identity
calls (`ensure!`) and `poll` in `streams.rs` — do what the clause says. D066
also says `MaybeUninit::assume_init` "follows a zeroed or kernel-filled struct,
matching its contract", while `identity/macos.rs::info<T>` zero-fills a generic
`T` without stating which `T` makes that valid. D020 keeps the statements and
details of D066 and D095 unchanged.

## Chosen

A successor for the two clauses only. The three best-effort sites in
`jobs/process.rs` deliberately discard the libc result; their soundness rests on
the remaining D066 argument — arguments built to the libc signature, no aliasing,
no borrowed pointer outliving the call — and each carries `// DECISION: D113`
in place of D066 or D095. The return-and-`errno` clause of D066 and D095 holds
for the checking sites, which keep their references. `restore_default_signals`
keeps `// DECISION: D095` on the function, which D095 introduced.
`identity/macos.rs::info<T>` requires `T` to be a libc plain-data struct whose
every bit pattern, the all-zero one included, is a valid value — today
`proc_bsdinfo` and `proc_taskinfo`; the zero-fill carries `// DECISION: D113`,
and a new `T` must meet the precondition. In the same delivery both PID
conversions in `jobs/process.rs` use `i32::try_from`, as `group.rs` does,
instead of `as i32`.

## Rejected

Editing D066 or D095 in place, which D020 forbids. Checking `errno` at the
best-effort sites: no caller can act on the failure, and the card keeps their
behavior. A marker trait bounding `T` for two call sites, an abstraction the
card does not ask for where a recorded precondition at the zero-fill suffices.
An invariant: the soundness and precondition are reviewed judgements, not a
statically checkable oracle (norm-or-choice).

## Rationale

The unsafe-justification rule (D065) checks that a reference exists, not that
its text matches the code (D059), so a justification that over-promises passes
silently. Pointing each best-effort site at an accurate statement, and the
zero-fill at its precondition, makes the referenced reason true without
changing any behavior.

## Consequences

`jobs/process.rs` no longer references D066; its three `unsafe` blocks reference
D113 and `restore_default_signals` references D095. `identity/macos.rs` adds one
D113 reference at the zero-fill; its `unsafe` blocks keep D066. Behavior for
every valid PID is unchanged. D066 and D095 remain current for their checking
sites and their other choices; D113 supersedes only their return-and-`errno`
clause for the three best-effort sites and states the `info<T>` precondition
(B051 AC1–AC3).
