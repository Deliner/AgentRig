# D007 — Structural size policy

## Context

Large files and crowded directories can conceal mixed ownership, but numeric size alone does not prove poor structure.

## Chosen

Warn at soft limits, fail at hard limits, and route source findings to behavior-preserving decomposition guidance.

## Rejected

Ignore growth or satisfy limits through compression and arbitrary splitting.

## Rationale

Tiered feedback prompts reassessment without treating every warning as a mandatory refactor.

## Consequences

The repository checker reports size findings and the focused skills govern justified decomposition.
