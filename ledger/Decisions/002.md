# D002 — One repository policy checker

## Context

Decision links, invariant links, and structural limits require consistent validation.

## Chosen

Use one standard-library checker with focused parser and size-policy modules.

## Rejected

Rely on prose review or distribute the same validation across consumers.

## Rationale

One owner prevents policy drift and keeps failures reproducible.

## Consequences

The quality gate and pre-commit hook invoke the same checker.
