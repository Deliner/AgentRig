---
name: norm-or-choice
description: Distinguish contextual decisions from executable invariants.
---

A choice selects among alternatives in a stated context: record Context, Chosen, Rejected, Rationale and Consequences. A norm asserts an observable predicate: link an exact executable oracle. If both apply, record both and their relationship. Supersede choices explicitly; preserve their committed identity and detail history. Current application links follow actual implementation ownership. Change invariant predicates together with their decision and tests. Routine Plan and State updates do not create a decision unless they change policy. External requirements without a repository oracle remain attributed instructions rather than pretend invariants.
